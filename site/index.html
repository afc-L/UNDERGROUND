Combat

How it hits

Fast, readable, arcade combat. The harder the hit, the louder everything gets. Clean hits draw blood that stays on the mat, and it can be switched off in Settings.

Impact you can feel

Every strike has an impact level from 1 to 6. Higher levels add more hit sparks, a longer hit-stop, harder camera shake, heavier sound and a bigger crowd reaction.

The biggest hits play a short cinematic, then hand control straight back to you.

HIT→SLOW MOTION→SHAKE→IMPACT→REACTION→NORMAL SPEED

Knockdowns and the count

Clean hits wear down a fighter's balance. Break it with a heavy shot and they hit the mat. The camera drops low, the crowd roars and the referee starts counting.

If you're the one on the floor, mash attack to get up sooner. There are no T.K.O.s and no judges: when the clock runs out it's sudden death, and the fight ends when someone dies.

An opponent that reads you

Each opponent blocks, dodges, counters and manages stamina at its own difficulty level. On hard, the AI watches your habits.

If you turtle, it goes after your guard with heavies. If you swing wildly, it sits back and counters. If you dodge a lot, it switches to safe jabs. If you're hurt, it presses harder.

Before you step in

Controls

All fighting is on the keyboard; the mouse only moves the camera. Lock-on is on by default, so movement circles your opponent.

WASD
Move, relative to the camera
Mouse
Camera only. Mouse buttons don't attack.
J
Light attack, chain up to 4
K
Heavy attack or combo finisher
Space
Block. Tap it just before a hit to parry.
Shift
Dodge. Hold it to sprint.
Q
Toggle lock-on
E
Special attack, when the meter is full (L also works)
Esc
Pause

The combo tree

  1. Light string
    JAB→CROSS→HOOK→UPPERCUT
  2. Kicks
    JAB / CROSS+ K →ROUNDHOUSE
    HOOK / UPPERCUT+ K →SPINNING HEEL KICK
  3. Counters

    A perfect block or a dodge through an attack opens a short window. Your next strike lands as a COUNTER for 1.5× damage.

  4. Underground Breaker

    The meter fills from hits, counters, parries and perfect dodges. The special can't be interrupted and always knocks down, then takes a long time to recharge.
